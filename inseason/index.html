ℹ️ My Lineup Strategist User Guide

Welcome to My Lineup Strategist! This tool is designed to eliminate start/sit headaches and bring clarity to your weekly fantasy football lineup decisions.

1. Syncing Leagues

Navigate to the Setup tab and enter your Sleeper Username and League ID. The tool will auto-import your team's players as well as your league's specific starting roster requirements (including Superflex).

2. Uploading Custom Rankings

Upload custom rankings from your favorite analyst in either .CSV or native .XLSX Excel format. Both top-to-bottom standard formats and side-by-side positional formats are supported automatically!

3. Thursday Night Football (TNF) Best Practice

Always try to keep your FLEX slots open for Sunday and Monday games! If you have a player playing on Thursday night, swap them into a primary positional slot (RB, WR, or TE) to maintain maximal roster flexibility over the weekend.
