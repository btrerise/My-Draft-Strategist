My Lineup Strategist Guide

Welcome to My Lineup Strategist! This tool is designed to eliminate start/sit headaches and bring clarity to your weekly fantasy football lineup decisions.

Syncing & Adding Leagues

Navigate to the Setup tab and enter your Sleeper Username and League ID to sync your roster automatically. If you don't use Sleeper, tap "Create Manual League" and add your players one-by-one.

Uploading Custom Rankings

Upload custom rankings from your favorite analyst in either .CSV or native .XLSX Excel format. Both top-to-bottom standard formats and side-by-side positional formats are supported automatically!

Upload Rest-of-Season (ROS) rankings on the Roster tab, and Weekly Matchup rankings on the Lineup tab.

Swapping & Optimizing

The Lineup Optimizer will do the heavy lifting for you based on the Weekly Rankings you provide. However, you can manually override it by tapping ⇄ Swap on any player to switch them with someone else (even players on the bench!). Tap ⚡ Optimize Lineup to revert back to the algorithmic ideal.

Exporting & Sharing

Once your lineup is set, tap the 📸 Export button to generate a clean, high-resolution screenshot of your Optimal Lineup to share with friends or post on social media!
