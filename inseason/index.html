My Lineup Strategist Guide

Welcome to My Lineup Strategist! This tool is designed to eliminate start/sit headaches and bring clarity to your weekly fantasy football lineup decisions.

1. Syncing & Adding Leagues

Navigate to the Setup tab and enter your Sleeper Username and League ID to sync your roster automatically. If you don't use Sleeper, tap "Create Manual League" and add your players one-by-one.

2. Uploading Custom Rankings

Upload custom rankings from your favorite analyst in either .CSV or native .XLSX Excel format. Both top-to-bottom standard formats and side-by-side positional formats are supported automatically!

3. Swapping & Optimizing

The Lineup Optimizer will do the heavy lifting for you. However, you can manually override it by tapping ⇄ Swap on any player to switch them with someone else (even players on the bench!). Tap ⚡ Optimize to revert back to the algorithmic ideal.
