My Lineup Strategist Guide

Welcome to My Lineup Strategist! This tool is designed to eliminate start/sit headaches and bring clarity to your weekly fantasy football lineup decisions.

1 Syncing & Adding Leagues

Navigate to the Dashboard tab and enter your Sleeper Username and League ID to sync your roster automatically. You can manage multiple leagues and switch between them using the header dropdown or the League Command Center. If you don't use Sleeper, tap "Create Manual" and add players one-by-one.

2 Uploading Custom Rankings & SoS

Upload custom analyst rankings in either .CSV or native .XLSX Excel format.

Upload Rest-of-Season (ROS) rankings on the Roster tab, and Weekly Matchup rankings on the Lineup tab. You can also upload or manually input a Strength of Schedule (SoS) matrix in the Roster tab to instantly generate color-coded matchup gradients on player cards. Note: If you upload a generic rankings CSV that contains a 'Matchup' or 'SoS' column, the app will automatically extract it for you.

3 Lineup Optimizer & Early Games

The Lineup Optimizer generates your highest-projected starting lineup based on your Weekly Rankings. Lock foundational starters with or tap ⇄ Swap to manually override positions. Select teams playing in early windows (TNF, London) under Early Games to flag them with an EARLY badge across your lineup before roster locks.

4 Scout & Waivers

The Scout tab is your interactive transaction hub. Paste waiver wire recommendations or trade targets. The app cross-references your Sleeper Sync data to show whether players are available on waivers, on your roster, or owned by opponents, alongside side-by-side Weekly and ROS ranks.

5 Trade Finder & Market Disconnects

Pull live market consensus values from FantasyCalc or LeagueLogs (or upload a market CSV). Use the Trade Finder to filter by flat rank gaps or percentage shifts to instantly uncover high-value buy-low targets and overvalued sell-high assets relative to your custom ROS board.

6 Positional Power Rankings

Found on the Scout tab, this tool evaluates every team's positional strength based on your custom ROS rankings or live market data. It generates a heatmap table of your league's rosters—hover or tap on any positional rank to see the specific players driving that score. Use this to quickly find ideal trade partners who are strong where you are weak!

7 Exporting & Sharing

Once your lineup is finalized, tap the Export button to generate a clean, high-resolution screenshot of your Optimal Lineup to share with friends or post on social media!
