My Lineup Strategist Guide

Welcome to My Lineup Strategist! This tool is designed to eliminate start/sit headaches and bring clarity to your weekly fantasy football lineup decisions.

1 Syncing & Adding Leagues

Navigate to the Setup tab (via the Menu) and enter your Sleeper Username and League ID to sync your roster automatically. If you don't use Sleeper, tap "Create Manual League" and add your players one-by-one.

2 Uploading Custom Rankings & SoS

Upload custom rankings from your favorite analyst in either .CSV or native .XLSX Excel format.

Upload Rest-of-Season (ROS) rankings on the Roster tab, and Weekly Matchup rankings on the Lineup tab. You can also upload or manually input a Strength of Schedule (SoS) matrix in the Roster tab to instantly generate visual matchup gradients on player cards! Note: If you upload a generic rankings CSV that already contains a 'Matchup' or 'SoS' column, the app will automatically extract it for you.

3 Scout & Waivers

The Scout tab is your interactive transaction hub. Paste a list of recommended waiver wire pickups or trade targets. The app will cross-reference your Sleeper Sync data to tell you immediately if they are available on waivers, on your roster, or owned by a specific opponent. It will also display their Weekly and ROS ranks side-by-side to help you weigh short-term vs. long-term value.

4 Swapping & Optimizing

The Lineup Optimizer will do the heavy lifting for you based on the Weekly Rankings you provide. However, you can manually override it by tapping ⇄ Swap on any player to switch them with someone else. Tap Optimize Lineup to revert back to the algorithmic ideal.

5 Exporting & Sharing

Once your lineup is set, tap the Export button to generate a clean, high-resolution screenshot of your Optimal Lineup to share with friends or post on social media!
