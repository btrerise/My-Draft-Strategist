How to Use My Draft Strategist

1. Load Rankings

Upload your custom rankings via CSV or Excel. Ensure your file has a 'Name' column. Or, use the Quick-Start option to automatically load live Market Value rankings from LeagueLogs.

2. Live Market Value (ADP)

Once custom rankings are loaded, click "Fetch Market Value" to pull live market data from LeagueLogs (incorporating ADP and platform-wide market trends) based on your specific league settings. The app will automatically assign Rookie badges (purple 'R') to first-year players in this process.

3. Sleeper Integration (Optional)

Users of other platforms can disregard this step and manually input draft pick selections on the Tracker tab. Enter your Sleeper Username and Draft ID to sync live draft picks. You can hit the Sync button manually, or toggle "Live Auto-Sync" to have the board update automatically every 3 seconds.

4. The Tracker

This is your primary view for drafting. It dynamically crosses off drafted players and leaves only the available pool.

Position Grid & Tiers (e.g., 'QB T2 (3)'): Use the position buttons to filter by position. The text 'T2 (3)' means that the highest available Tier for that position is currently Tier 2, and there are exactly 3 players remaining in that tier. Use this to quickly identify positional runs and drop-offs.

Value / Reach Badge: On each player card, you'll see a dynamic value badge based on your rankings. If a player's Custom Rank is lower (better) than the current overall pick number, it shows a green value badge (e.g., +12 Value). If they are ranked much higher than the current pick, it shows a red reach indicator (e.g., -8 Reach).

Inline Editor: Tap the pencil icon (✏️) on any player card to quickly adjust their Rank, Tier, Team, or Bye Week without leaving the Tracker.

5. The Draft Board

A full-bleed grid that scales dynamically to your league size (e.g., 10-team, 12-team, 14-team) to show exactly who drafted whom, round-by-round. Supports both live Sleeper sync and manual offline pick tracking!

6. Roster Limits & Bye Weeks

Set your starting lineup requirements in the Setup tab. The Team tab will automatically slot your drafted players into their correct positions and calculate your roster limits. Toggle on Bye Week warnings to get instant alerts if 3 or more of your starters share a bye.

7. Call Outs & Stacks

On the Setup tab, paste lists of names to highlight them throughout the app as Targets (🟢), Avoids (🔴), or Dart Throws (🔵). Turn on "Highlight Stacks" to automatically place a "🔥 Stack" badge on available WRs or TEs if you have already drafted their Quarterback.

8. Install the App (Optional)

For the best experience, you can install this app directly to your home screen! If your browser supports it, tap the "Install App" button on the Setup screen or use your browser's menu options (like "Add to Home Screen"). This will give you a full-screen, native app experience.
