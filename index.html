How to Use My Draft Strategist

1 Load Rankings

Upload your custom rankings via CSV or Excel. Ensure your file has a 'Name' column. Or, use the Quick-Start option to automatically load live Market Value rankings directly from the LeagueLogs market.

2 Live Market Value (ADP)

Once custom rankings are loaded, click "Fetch Market Value" to pull live market data from LeagueLogs or Sleeper. The app incorporates ADP and platform-wide market trends based on your specific league settings. The app will also automatically assign Rookie badges (R) to first-year players during this process.

3 Add / Sync Draft Profiles

You can maintain multiple draft profiles at once and swap between them using the header dropdown.

  • Live Sync: Users drafting on Sleeper can enter their Username and Draft ID to sync live draft picks. You can hit the Sync button manually or toggle "Live Auto-Sync" to have the board update automatically every 3 seconds.
  • Manual Drafts: Users of other platforms can click "Create Manual Draft" and manually input pick selections on the Tracker tab.

4 Roster Limits, Settings & Analytics

Set your starting lineup requirements in the Setup tab. The Team tab will automatically slot drafted players into their correct positions and calculate roster limits.

  • Highlight Stacks Turn on "Highlight Stacks" to automatically place a Stack badge on available WRs or TEs if you have already drafted their Quarterback (or vice versa).
  • Bye Week Warnings: Toggle this to get instant alerts if 3 or more of your starters share a bye week.
  • T-Score Analytics: Toggle this on to overlay predictive WR scoring models, highlighting receivers with labels like Elite, High, Strong, Quality, or Boom.
  • UI Controls: You can also toggle player headshots on the Draft Board or enable the "Collapse Player Cards" setting to save screen space on mobile devices.

5 Call Outs & Stacks

On the Setup tab, paste lists of names to permanently highlight them throughout the app as Targets (), Avoids (), or Dart Throws ().

6 The Tracker

This is your primary view for drafting. It dynamically crosses off drafted players and leaves only the available pool.

  • Position Grid & Tiers: Use the position buttons to filter by position. The text T2 (3) means that the highest available Tier for that position is currently Tier 2, and there are exactly 3 players remaining in that tier. Use this to quickly identify positional runs and drop-offs. Additionally, you can tap multiple position buttons at the top (e.g., RB and WR) to view specific positional runs side-by-side.
  • Injury Status: Players with injury reports will have a visible badge indicating their status.
  • Queue: Tap the ★ on any player card to pin them to your personal Queue at the top of the Tracker. You can drag-and-drop or use the arrows to reorder your targets.
  • Affinity Color Labels: Tap the circle icon () next to the Queue Star on any player card to cycle through 5 custom color labels (Green, Yellow, Orange, Red, Purple) to organize and highlight your target players on the fly.
  • Rookie Badge: First-year players will have a visible badge indicating their rookie status.
  • Stacks: Players who are part of a stack (e.g., a QB and his WR) will have a visible badge indicating their stack status, if selected on the Setup tab.
  • Value / Reach Badge: On each player card, you'll see a dynamic value badge based on your rankings. +12 Value means their Custom Rank is 12 spots better than the current pick. -8 Reach means drafting them now is a reach.
  • Inline Editor: Tap the pencil icon () on any player card to quickly adjust their Rank, Tier, Team, or Bye Week without leaving the Tracker. You can also select the "★" button to add them to your queue.
  • Undo: If a mistake is made, drafted players can be returned to the pool using the "Undo" buttons.
  • Missing Players: If you are using Sleeper Live Sync, the app will automatically generate a player card if someone drafts a Kicker or Defense that wasn't in your rankings. If you are drafting manually, a player must be in your uploaded rankings to be drafted.

7 The Team Tab & Exporting

This tab shows your starting lineup and bench.

  • Draft Recap: Once your roster is filled, the app generates a Post-Draft Recap. This includes your Draft Archetype (e.g., Hero RB, Zero RB, Elite TE), Positional Grades (A+ through F based on Draft Value Efficiency), and highlights your Biggest Steal and Biggest Reach.
  • Export Team: Click the "Export" button to capture an image of your drafted lineup and optional recap analysis to save or share to social media.

8 The Draft Board

A full-bleed matrix that scales dynamically to your league size (e.g., 10-team, 12-team) to show exactly who drafted whom, round-by-round. It supports both live Sleeper sync and manual offline pick tracking!

9 Install the App

For the best experience, you can install this app directly to your home screen! If your browser supports it, tap the "Install App" button on the Setup screen or use your browser's menu options (like "Add to Home Screen") to get a full-screen, native app experience.

10 Keyboard Shortcuts

Draft like a power-user with these global keyboard shortcuts (active whenever you aren't typing in a text box):

  • / (Forward Slash): Instantly jump to the Player Search bar.
  • Escape: Clear the search bar and reset the board.
  • Spacebar / Enter: Draft the currently focused player.
  • A, Q, R, W, T: Instantly filter the Tracker by position (All, QB, RB, WR, TE).
  • 1, 2, 3, 4, 5: Instantly switch tabs (Setup, Tracker, Team, Board, Guide).
